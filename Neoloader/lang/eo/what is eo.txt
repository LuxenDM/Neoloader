eo is the lang-code for Esperanto, a 'constructed language'. This is intended as a low-barrier-of-entry second or third language people can learn.

Why is it supported here? Mostly as a showcase of language support structure and the ability to handle translation pipelines in Neoloader. I also REALLY like the idea of making communication easier for _everyone_. Look forward to proper support being added in the full release of Neoloader v7.0.0!
